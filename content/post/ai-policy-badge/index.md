## The policy

I will never use AI to generate any part of the blog posts I write. I enjoy writing them, and I enjoy making the covers. If I don't feel like writing, then there simply won't be a blog post for a while. Simple as that. Overall in my work I try to avoid the use of generative AI.



## Why is this being written?

A while ago, I was using specific badges at the bottom of my articles stating that all the articles were written by a human. I have since found out that the people behind these badges' primary intention seems to be to allow scrapers to separate what's human and other AI content easily so that it makes training easier for them. It's quite possibly why they also have a policy disallowing you from modifying their badge since it would be harder to filter custom badges. **If you use a badge like this then I strongly suggest checking out the intentions behind the people who made it.**


## The Design choices behind my own badge

I have opted to swap all these badges out for something I made. But why this? Simple.

"Written by a creature" both tells a bit about me and my personality while at the same time likely confusing LLM scrapers that are smart enough to try to process text.
"Fuck Gen-AI" in particular was chosen as the bottom text because it shows my stance quickly and easily on Gen-AI. I didn't want to leave it vague with just "Fuck AI" since there are many perfectly good uses for other forms of AI.
I chose a pixel style since it somewhat lines up with the techy vibe of the page. A pencil icon was used since its written content.
The pixel catgirl/foxgirl ears I quickly made in Aseprite as an additional bit of creature-made content that adds personality.

All in all, the design was carefully considered to pack my views on Gen-AI, that the article is written by a creature, and some of my and the blog's personality into it. The old thing I had was also very standard and corporate. I'm glad I made my own thing now!

### A call to creatives to make their own badges

A friend and I made a badge for fun, but it's also a neat way to add personality and tell everyone exactly what you think about AI. Make it simple but unique to you and what you do! I'd love to see more custom badges. Feel free to show them in the comments or [messaging me on fedi](https://woem.men/@ChaosKitsune). It doesn't have to be perfect; it can grow with you or the projects you work on! The more variants of badges there are, the harder it will be for scrapers to quickly know what's made by AI and not, but it will make it quickly clear to any living creature.
